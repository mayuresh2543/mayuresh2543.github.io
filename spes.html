Clean Flash (First Time Install)

1
Connect & Verify

Connect your device to the PC and verify the connection:

adb devices

It should show your device ID/serial number.

2
Enter Bootloader

Reboot your device into Fastboot mode:

adb reboot bootloader
3
Boot Recovery

Boot into recovery image (do not flash):

fastboot boot recovery.img

Your device should now boot into recovery mode automatically.

4
Install ROM

Transfer the ROM zip to your device storage if you haven't already.

  1. Tap on Install.
  2. Navigate to the folder where you saved the zip.
  3. Select the ROM file.
  4. Swipe to flash.
5
GApps (Vanilla Only)

Skip this step if your ROM includes GApps.

  1. After flashing ROM, go back and Format Data.
  2. Reboot to Recovery again.
  3. Flash your GApps zip.
6
Format & Reboot
  1. Go to Wipe / Factory Reset and select Format Data.
  2. If it fails: Go to Home > Advanced > Reboot to Recovery, then try formatting again.
  3. Reboot System.

Update Guide

Option 1: Dirty Flash (Keep Data)

  1. Boot into Recovery.
  2. Tap Install and select the new ROM zip.
  3. Swipe to flash.
  4. Wipe Cache/Dalvik (Optional).
  5. Reboot System.

Do not format data for a dirty flash.
